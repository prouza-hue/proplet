Půlměsíc: gold carved crescent with a cobalt star; replaces 14-day loyalty art.
Vyzvat kamaráda: broad crossed ivory/cobalt swords, red and blue grips.
Klidný režim: jade leaf with a turquoise wave and gold central vein.

Created with the built-in image generation tool, approved by Pavel, faithfully converted to path-only SVG. No redesign or new generation is needed to rebuild.

Verified at 128, 32 and 24px on light/dark backgrounds. Dynamic UI decoration preserves accessible labels and avoids same-value DOM writes. Preview only; XP thresholds, reward predicates and gameplay unchanged. These three items complete the deferred artwork TODO from September 8.
