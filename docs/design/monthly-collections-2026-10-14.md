# Monthly collection direction and December assets

Approved direction: gold King, silver Knight, bronze Prince, regardless of historical noble precedence. New seasonal characters every month, not recolored medals. Tactile letterpress-workshop style. Copy is a short natural Czech aside spoken by the character, situational humor, no pompous slogan or accidental sexual double meaning.
